Rogue Rat Brand Design & Guidelines

Tools: Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign

Year: 2025

Project Overview

Rogue Rat attracts the target audience by celebrating imperfection, experimentation, and freedom. The brand embraces the raw, unpolished energy of street culture, creating a space where individuality is not just accepted but encouraged. By leaning into authenticity rather than perfection, Rogue Rat connects emotionally with skaters who see their boards, their style, and their art as extensions of who they are. It speaks to young riders who value originality, rebellion, and creativity, offering a brand identity that feels real, relatable, and rooted in the streets.

Deliverables

  • Corporate Mark
  • Brand Colours and Typefaces
  • Icons and Patterns
  • Stationary
  • Merchandise
